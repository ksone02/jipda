# Product definition tools

## Press release and FAQ
Write the future announcement before building.
- **Press release, in order:** heading (one sentence), subheading (customer and benefit, one sentence), summary, problem from the customer's view, solution and how it differs from current options, one quote from the team and one from a customer, how to get started.
- **External FAQ:** what customers will ask.
- **Internal FAQ:** what it costs, what is hard, what could block it.
- **Check:** Is the customer clearly defined? Is the benefit worth switching for? If the release is dull, the idea is weak. Fix the idea, not the prose.

## Opportunity tree
Requires a clear outcome and real customer input. Without either, say so.
1. Put one outcome at the top.
2. Map customer moments and the needs and pains in each.
3. Pick **one** opportunity.
4. Generate solutions for that opportunity only, then pick 3.
5. Break each into assumptions and test the riskiest first.
6. Decide: discard, refine, or pick a new opportunity.

Show: outcome → opportunities → chosen one → 3 solutions → riskiest assumption each.
