# Market tools

## Market sizing
Use public numbers and label every input as sourced or assumed.
- **Bottom-up:** customers × price × purchase frequency.
- **Total:** everyone who could buy. **Serviceable:** the part you can reach. **Obtainable:** the realistic share.
- **Top-down cross-check:** a published market total narrowed to your slice. If the two differ a lot, find out why before trusting either.
- A large total alone says little. The obtainable number is what informs the decision.

## Needs-based segments
Group people by the benefit they seek, not by demographics.
1. List the needs and buying behaviors for this problem.
2. Group those that occur together. Without data, mark groups as hypotheses to check with real users.
3. Profile each group: what makes it identifiable and reachable.
4. Test each: identifiable, large enough, reachable, responsive, actionable.
5. Pick a primary segment, and say which are not targets.
